Privacy notice

Last updated: October 27, 2026

This notice explains, in plain language, what personal information ZORADOX collects through this website, why we collect it and how you can control it. ZORADOX is based in Canada and works with clients worldwide.

What we collect

  • Contact form: your name, email address, company (if you add one), project type and message.
  • Intro call booking: the details you enter when you book through Cal.com, such as your name, email address, the time you choose and any notes you add.
  • Email: your email address and anything you write when you email us.

This website doesn’t use analytics or advertising cookies. Like most websites, our server keeps basic technical logs (such as IP address, browser type and pages requested) for security and troubleshooting.

Why we collect it

We use your information only to reply to your enquiry, hold the intro call you booked, and discuss or quote your project. We don’t sell your information, and we don’t add you to a mailing list.

Who processes it

We use a small number of trusted services to run this website and our communication:

  • Web3Forms delivers contact form messages to our inbox. Web3Forms privacy policy
  • Cal.com handles intro call bookings. Cal.com privacy policy
  • Zoho Mail hosts our email, including form messages and replies. Zoho privacy policy
  • Hostinger hosts the server this website runs on. Hostinger privacy policy

These services may store or process your information outside Canada, under their own privacy policies.

How long we keep it

This applies to form messages, emails and booking details.

  • Enquiries that don’t become projects: kept for up to 1 year after our last contact, then deleted.
  • Client records: kept for the duration of the work, and for as long as legal and accounting obligations require.
  • Server logs: kept briefly, for security and troubleshooting.

Your choices

You can ask to see the personal information we hold about you, correct it, or have it deleted. Email us at contact@zoradox.com and we’ll respond as soon as we can.

Privacy contact

ZORADOX is responsible for the personal information described here. Our privacy contact can be reached at contact@zoradox.com.

Changes to this notice

If we change how we handle your information, we’ll update this page and the date at the top.
