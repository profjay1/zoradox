// 07 — FAQ

Questions, answered.

Short, straight answers. Something else on your mind? Get in touch.

How long does a project take?

It depends on the scope. A focused website takes less time than a complex web application. We agree on a realistic timeline together during planning, before development starts.

How much does a project cost?

Every project is quoted individually, based on its scope and the way of working that suits you. The free intro call is the first step: once we understand your needs, we can prepare a quote.

What happens in the free intro call?

In 30 minutes, we learn about your goals and what you need, then recommend the way of working that fits your project. There’s no obligation.

Do you work with clients outside Canada?

Yes. We work with clients across Canada and worldwide. We work remotely and arrange meeting times to suit your time zone.

How will we stay updated during the project?

Through regular check-ins where you see your project take shape, so you can give feedback as we go.

Can you improve or rebuild our existing website?

Yes. We start by reviewing what you have, then recommend whether it’s better to improve your current site or rebuild it.

What do you need from us to get started?
  • Your goals for the project
  • Any existing content or brand materials
  • Access to your current systems, if relevant
  • One point of contact for decisions and feedback
Do you offer support after launch?

Yes. We offer post-launch support once your project is live. For longer-term care, our Ongoing support model covers updates, regular check-ups and improvements.

Will we get documentation?

Yes. We document what we build and hand it over properly, so your team can understand and maintain it.

Which technologies do you use?

Proven, well-supported tools, chosen to suit each project. See the technologies we work with.
